=== Slide 7 ===
Research Hypotheses
7
H1: Context can recover missing labels
Article context may clarify actors and references.
H2: Context can introduce extra errors
Other events may influence the current prediction.
H3: Selective context may improve the trade-off
A gate may retain useful context while limiting interference.
H1/H2 mechanisms need case analysis. H3 remains untested.
