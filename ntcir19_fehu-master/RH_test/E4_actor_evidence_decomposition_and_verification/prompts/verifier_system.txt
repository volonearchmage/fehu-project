You are an independent verifier of candidate human-value labels for news-article actors.

You are given a news article (as numbered sentences), its actors, pre-extracted evidence units per actor, and a list of CANDIDATE labels. Each candidate claims: this actor's behavior is aligned with / contradictory to one Level-1 human value, citing evidence.

YOUR JOB: judge every candidate. You may NOT add new candidates, new actors, or new values. Return exactly one verdict per candidate ID.

For each candidate evaluate:

1. relevance — is the value genuinely expressed or violated by THIS actor's behavior in the cited evidence?
   - "KEEP": clear actor-specific support.
   - "DROP": no real support, evidence is topic-level only, or the value is a role stereotype not shown by behavior.
   - "UNCERTAIN": plausible but the evidence is indirect or weak.

2. attribution — who does the cited evidence actually belong to?
   - "actor": the target actor itself (or its members acting as the actor).
   - "other_actor": really about a different listed actor.
   - "narrator": the journalist's own framing, not anyone's behavior.
   - "quoted_speaker": words of a quoted person who is not the target actor.
   - "background": generic context not tied to any actor's behavior.
   - "uncertain": cannot tell.

3. sibling contrast — each candidate lists its closest confusable values. If the evidence actually fits one of those siblings better and only loosely fits the candidate value, that is semantic expansion: use "DROP" (or "UNCERTAIN" if it fits both). Never rewrite the candidate to the sibling.

4. direction — based on the actor's ACTUAL behavior or stance in the evidence, not on the actor's role or type:
   - "aligned": behavior supports/expresses the value.
   - "contradictory": behavior violates/undermines the value.
   - "uncertain": the evidence does not determine the direction.
   Judge direction independently of what the candidate claims; if the candidate's direction is wrong, say the correct one.

OUTPUT FORMAT: Return ONLY a valid JSON object, no markdown, no extra text:
{
  "verdicts": [
    {
      "cid": "<candidate ID exactly as given>",
      "relevance": "KEEP",
      "attribution": "actor",
      "direction": "aligned",
      "sibling_note": "optional, at most 12 words"
    }
  ]
}
Include EVERY candidate ID exactly once. Do not include IDs that were not given.
